The first run dated some operations by the wrong day (the same operation on two or three
neighbouring dates); the second sorted by `getBoundingClientRect().top`, which is the same for
every header `<li>` on T-Bank, and duplicated operations across many days. The last two runs
produced the **same multiset** of (date, title, amount): the 69 rows skipped by the third run had
been read in earlier rounds, and the carry dates them without adding or moving anything.

The header carry above was replaced in round 2 of this PR by overlap alignment: the leading rows
of a round are dated only when the window overlaps the previous one (the longest run of at least
2 rows, headers included, that ends the previous window and starts this one), so an unparsed or
skipped day header can no longer make them inherit the wrong day. A first version looked the
leading block up anywhere in the previous window (last occurrence): its live run (6a6d0a7, 326
rows, 300 s) dated a recurring train ticket by a neighbouring day and added it a second time on
2026-03-31 (the day's header total showed only one); anchoring the match at both window ends fixed
it. Rows with no such overlap are skipped, reported in `headerless_rows` and in a warning, and the
run is marked incomplete.

The shipped code (329a7fd, run at 12:01 MSK) gave 325 rows, 0 headerless, 0 date inversions. Against
the 662015b run the only differences are a taxi ride made on the day of the run (2026-10-27, not
yet in the earlier run) and one 10 ₽ row of 2025-12-31, the day before `stop_before_date`, which
the scroll may or may not reach (that day is outside the requested range). On this run 86 days show
an expense total in their header (2025-12-31 no longer does): 79 match to the ruble, the other 7
are the same 7 days explained below, and no day is short.

An earlier summary in this PR said "85 of 86 days". That used a looser criterion — days whose
difference was explainable by transfers counted as matching, and one day without its total in the
captured header text was missed. With the strict criterion (exact match versus an explained
difference) the 662015b run gives **80 exact + 7 explained of 87 days**; the tables below use
that strict number.

## Check against the cabinet's own numbers

A T-Bank day header shows the day's expense total when the day has expenses ("27 октября−1 234 ₽",
whole rubles). For each such day the collected negative amounts were summed and compared:

- days in the output: 138; days whose header showed a total: 87;
- equal to the ruble (header shows whole rubles, kopecks truncated): **80**;
- different: **7**, each by exactly one collected operation that T-Bank leaves out of its own day
  total — 5 top-ups of a brokerage account (category «Переводы»), 1 «Покупка по QR» in
  «Финансы», 1 autopayment of 200 ₽ in «Другое»;
- days where the collected expenses are **smaller** than the header: **0** (no evidence of a
  missing operation).

Not checked numerically: income, and the 51 days without an expense total in the header.
Balances are not read by this profile.
